Questions, answered

How fast will I be live?

Most builds are live within 24–48 hours of your kickoff. Larger or custom projects can take a little longer — we'll tell you upfront.

How does the $100 new-customer offer work?

If you’re a new customer, we take $100 off your total purchase, applied on your first invoice — on any machine bundle, website plan or the Buy-Out Program. One per business; can’t be combined with other offers.

Do you charge sales tax?

Only on physical equipment. Card machines and other hardware are taxed at your county’s NY sales tax rate (for example 8.75% in Suffolk, 8.625% in Nassau). Website design, setup and online services aren’t subject to NY sales tax. Tax is shown as its own line on your invoice.

Do I have to get a card machine?

No. You can take just the website and online setup on its own — see the "Website only" pricing. The machine bundle is for businesses that also want to take card payments.

Is the website free with a machine?

Yes — with any machine package, your website and online setup are included free. The cloud software fee and monthly subscription still apply, as with all our services (see the fine print under pricing).

How do processing rates work?

Rates depend on your business type and volume. Send a recent statement and we'll review it free and show you your rate before you commit.

Do I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, website, and your Google, Apple and Facebook accounts are all in your name. We're added as managers.

What will I pay?

Every service has three parts: a one-time fee (setup or buy-out), a cloud software fee, and a monthly subscription. Your written quote shows all three before you sign. Your prices are locked in — the only exception is Buy-Out Program pricing, which can change only if the AI software it runs on goes up in price.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Anytime, on website-only plans. If you cancel, we'll help move your site to hosting you control.
